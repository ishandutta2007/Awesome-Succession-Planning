## 🌟 Top Revenue Recognition Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on ASC 606 / IFRS 15 Compliance, Deferred Revenue, SSP Allocation, Contract Modifications & Automated Revenue Schedules*

**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Revenue Recognition**. These systems automate the identification of performance obligations, allocation of transaction price (including SSP), timing of revenue recognition (point-in-time or over-time), contract modifications, variable consideration, and generation of compliant journal entries and waterfalls under ASC 606 and IFRS 15.

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosted billing, revenue schedule engines, Odoo modules, educational ASC 606/IFRS 15 implementations, and open monetization platforms that can support or feed revenue recognition workflows — ideal for finance engineers, SaaS companies, and organizations seeking transparency and control over complex revenue accounting.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## 💻 Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[erpnext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)  

  Open-source ERP system that includes accounting and deferred revenue capabilities.

- **[lago](https://github.com/getlago/lago)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getlago/lago?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/getlago/lago/stargazers)  

  Open-source metering, usage-based billing, and subscription management platform with revenue analytics that can feed downstream rev-rec processes.

- **[pentaho-kettle](https://github.com/pentaho/pentaho-kettle)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/pentaho/pentaho-kettle?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/pentaho/pentaho-kettle/stargazers)  

  Data integration tool useful for building custom ETL pipelines for revenue data before passing to GL.

- **[killbill](https://github.com/killbill/killbill)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/killbill/killbill?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/stargazers)  

  Mature open-source subscription billing and payments platform that provides the transactional foundation many teams extend for custom revenue recognition logic.

- **[unibee](https://github.com/unibee-billing/unibee)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/unibee-billing/unibee?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/unibee-billing/unibee/stargazers)

- **[ssi-revenue-recognition](https://github.com/open-synergy/ssi-revenue-recognition)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/open-synergy/ssi-revenue-recognition?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/open-synergy/ssi-revenue-recognition/stargazers)

- **[revbot](https://github.com/birchpoplar/revbot)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/birchpoplar/revbot?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/birchpoplar/revbot/stargazers)

- **[Odoo-15-accounting](https://github.com/Tiggil/Odoo-15-accounting)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Tiggil/Odoo-15-accounting?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Tiggil/Odoo-15-accounting/stargazers)
